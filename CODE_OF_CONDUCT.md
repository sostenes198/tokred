# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is a short form; the linked text is the reference.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by our mistakes, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community as a whole, not only for us as individuals.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel of the repository), and when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and temporarily or permanently ban a person for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately: use the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or reach the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/sostenes198). All reports are reviewed and handled promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the person who reported are respected.

Maintainers follow the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant: correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban, in proportion to the impact.
